How it works

Three steps, no guesswork.

01

Add your cards

Tell Phronix which cards you carry — just the product, like "Commercial Bank Visa Signature." Never a card number.

02

Search a merchant

Type where you're shopping, or browse by category — dining, travel, shopping, groceries.

Get the verdict

Phronix ranks every matching offer by real savings and tells you exactly which card to use, right now.

The name

Practical wisdom, right when it matters.

Phronix comes from phronesis (φρόνησις) — the Greek word for practical wisdom: knowing the right thing to do, right when it matters. That's the idea behind every card the app recommends.

Never collected

Phronix never collects your card number, CVV, PIN, expiry date, banking credentials, OTPs, or transaction history. Only the name of the card product you own is ever stored.
