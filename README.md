# Phronix

**Find the best card offer before you pay.**

Phronix is a mobile app that helps you decide which card to use, right when it matters. Add the cards you own, search where you're shopping, and Phronix matches live bank offers and hands you one ranked verdict — before you tap or swipe.

## What you get

- **Live bank offers** — hundreds of active promotions from Sri Lanka's leading banks, refreshed automatically.
- **Bill calculator** — enter the amount on your bill and see the exact rupee saving, not just an advertised percentage.
- **Saved offers** — bookmark deals you want to remember, with reminders before they expire.
- **Favourite merchants** — star the places you shop often and get told the moment a new deal lands there.
- **Category browsing** — dining, travel, shopping, groceries, and more, sorted by how much you'd actually save.
- **Lock & themes** — fingerprint or face unlock, with light and dark themes that follow your system or stay put.

## Privacy, by design

Phronix never collects your card number, CVV, PIN, expiry date, banking credentials, OTPs, or transaction history. Only the name of the card product you own is ever stored.

## The name

Phronix comes from *phronesis* (φρόνησις) — the Greek word for practical wisdom: knowing the right thing to do, right when it matters. That's the idea behind every card the app recommends.
